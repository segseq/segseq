Ways To Compete

🏅

Multi-Sport Madness

While Strava limits you to one sport per challenge, SegSeq lets you mix them up. Create your own Ironman, triathlon, or run-and-bike event!

🔁

Non-Consecutive Reps

Strava requires consecutive loops. SegSeq innovates by taking your best efforts on a segment over a given period, whether consecutive or not.

⛷️

Ski Resort Showdown

Explore a mountain and measure yourself on the slopes of a ski resort during your winter holidays.

📍

City Explorer

Turn sightseeing into a race. Connect iconic landmarks and discover the city at your own pace, but sprint the segments.
